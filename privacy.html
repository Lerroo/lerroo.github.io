Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What data we access

With your permission, hub accesses your Google Calendar data (calendars and events) through the Google Calendar API.

How we use it

The data is used only to display, create and update events in your own calendar at your request. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing and storage

hub does not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties. Calendar data is not stored on any server beyond what is needed to complete a request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke hub's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
